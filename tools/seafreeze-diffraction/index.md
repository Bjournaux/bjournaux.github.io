# SeaFreeze diffraction

Lattice parameters and synthetic powder diffractograms of the ices of H<sub>2</sub>O at any pressure and temperature, from the volumes computed with [SeaFreeze](/seafreeze/).
Click a point on the phase diagram: its stable ice is shown below the diagram, and the metastable ices SeaFreeze can evaluate there can be added. On a phase boundary or at a triple point, the coexisting ices are shown together.
