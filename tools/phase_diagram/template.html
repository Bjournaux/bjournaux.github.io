How it is computed. SeaFreeze 1.2.0b1 (liquid and vapour: water3; ices: Journaux et al. 2020) evaluated on a 583 × 251 pressure–temperature grid, then interpolated; boundaries and triple points are solved directly. Against direct SeaFreeze calls the stable phase always matches, density agrees within 2 × 10−5 and __PROP_ACCURACY__ For the fluid, Vp is the sound speed. Ices VII and VIII (above 2 GPa) are not included.
