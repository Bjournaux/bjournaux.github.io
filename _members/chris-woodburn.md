Chris Woodburn completed his PhD in the lab in 2025.
His [dissertation](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/researchworks/items/d4f89600-0988-4d5d-8d66-07e9a388e803/full) used high-pressure experiments to support the exploration of icy ocean worlds such as Europa and Titan: how biological molecules interact with high-pressure ices, a step toward detecting life, and how salts change the freezing of water in deep extraterrestrial oceans.
