Our discovery of hyperhydrated sodium chloride hydrates, stable at icy moon surface conditions and [published in PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2217125120), was covered by [CNN](https://us.cnn.com/2023/02/20/world/salty-ice-europa-ocean-moons-scn/index.html) and the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/02/25/science/space/salty-ice-moons.html).
