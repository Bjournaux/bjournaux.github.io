Our paper with F. Petricca and colleagues, [published in Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09818-x), shows that the strong tidal dissipation revealed by Cassini data precludes a subsurface ocean on Titan.

The study was covered by PBS / Associated Press, Smithsonian Magazine, Gizmodo, GeekWire, UW News, Le Figaro and Sciences et Avenir.
